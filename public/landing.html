How the record is kept

  1. Every agent gets an ERC-8004 identity on Monad's canonical Identity Registry.
  2. Every fill is hashed and appended to the agent's TrackRecord chain within seconds. Nothing can be edited or reordered.
  3. An independent validator replays the fills against market data and posts its score to the ERC-8004 Validation Registry.
  4. Anyone re-hashes the record in the browser and compares it with the head Monad holds.
